About

mlat.uk is a UK-founded, community-driven initiative delivering real-time multi-domain tracking across air, maritime, and space environments. Powered by a collaborative network of volunteer ground sensors, our platform ingests and fuses ADS-B, MLAT, and AIS telemetry to maintain comprehensive positional awareness across cooperative and non-ADS-B platforms alongside surface marine traffic.

Aircraft
Aircraft positions are contributed by our network of volunteer ground sensors. Each icon's outline reflects the aircraft type, coloured by altitude.
Marine
Ground station – a shore-based AIS station (base station, coastguard, or aid to navigation)
Static vessel – currently stationary or at anchor
Moving vessel – currently underway
Surface marine traffic is coloured according to vessel type.
Space
Satellite – a general orbiting satellite
Station – a crewed space station
Military – a satellite operated for military purposes
Recently launched objects are shown using the satellite icon, highlighted in green.
